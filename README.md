### 🔬 Profile & Research Direction

I work at the intersection of **deep learning theory, computer vision, and neural network efficiency**. My research investigates mechanisms for compressing high-capacity representation models (e.g., Vision Transformers and deep backbones) into low-latency, compute-efficient architectures suitable for real-time edge execution.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering · Noble University *(9.22 / 10 CGPA)*
- 🎯 **Primary Research:** Knowledge Distillation, Feature Compression, Edge Vision
- 💡 **Core Mindset:** Understanding mathematical and structural representations rather than treating deep learning frameworks as black boxes.
